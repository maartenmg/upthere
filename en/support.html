Frequently asked

Do I need an account?

No. Up There works without an account, email address or password. The app makes a random number for your phone and keeps it in the keychain.

How do I follow someone?

Ask them for a code from their own app (People › You › Share your flights) and enter it under People › Add someone. You can also add flights yourself, or a calendar link they gave you.

I don't get notifications.

Check iOS Settings › Up There › Notifications. In the app, every person has their own notification switch. Between 23:00 and 07:00 only a landing or a cancellation comes through; the rest waits until morning.

The times are wrong.

The app shows the scheduled times from the calendar or the flight data, and the live status once the flight is close. If something is off, email the flight number and the date and I will look into it.

How do I erase my data?

In the app: Settings › Erase my data. That removes everything of this phone from our server, at once and for good. More in the privacy policy.

How do I cancel Comfort?

Comfort is a subscription through your Apple Account. Cancel it in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or in the App Store › your profile › Subscriptions.
